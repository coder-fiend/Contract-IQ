About Contract IQ

AI-powered legal document understanding and assistance platform focused on Indian law.

🎯 Our Mission

Legal agreements and statutory provisions in India are often dense, opaque, and difficult for non-lawyers to evaluate. Contract IQ demystifies legal text by extracting operative terms, checking clauses against statutory benchmarks, and providing clear, actionable explanations grounded in verified Indian law.

⚖️ Authoritative Legal Knowledge Base

Contract IQ maintains a verified statutory repository comprising:

  • Indian Contract Act, 1872: Formation, non-compete restrictions (Sec 27), and breach damages (Sec 73, 74).
  • Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023: Commenced 1 July 2024, governing modern penal offences (Sec 316, 318, 351).
  • Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC): Retained for historical offences committed on or before 30 June 2024.
  • Consumer Protection Act, 2019: Deficiency in service, unfair contract terms (Sec 2(46)), and e-Daakhil filing.
  • Information Technology Act, 2000: Electronic contracts, cyber cheating, and data confidentiality.

🔒 Privacy & Document Isolation

Each user's uploaded agreement is assigned an isolated session token and stored in a private FAISS vector index. Uploaded documents are never intermingled, never made available to other sessions, and never used to train public models.

🛡️ Legal Safety & Ethical Safeguards

Contract IQ strictly enforces verification safeguards:

  • Never invents or fabricates legal sections or fictitious court precedents.
  • Distinguishes between civil breach and criminal offences.
  • Considers the date of alleged offences to distinguish BNS from IPC.
  • Does not guarantee legal outcomes or encourage illegal conduct.
